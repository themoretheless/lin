On64-bit targets, when allocated length is at most floor(u32::MAX/64) words, store logical capacity in the low32bits and exact cardinality in the high32bits. Growth/OR decode the old logical capacity before changing representation. AND/OR preserve fallback capacity. Debug continues to expose logical capacity, not packed metadata.
